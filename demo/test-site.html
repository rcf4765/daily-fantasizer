    Add the Chrome extension first
    
  
    ESPN has no sign-in for other websites. Like FantasyPros, The Daily Fantasizer uses a small Chrome extension to read your ESPN leagues after you sign in on ESPN's own page.
    
  
    1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
    2. Download the extension and unzip it.
    3. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
    4. Come back and press Link ESPN Fantasy account again.
